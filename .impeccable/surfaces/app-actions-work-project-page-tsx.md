# Surface brief: project case study (flagship: EazyCRM)

Scope: the project page at /work/:slug, starting with EazyCRM. Visitor mode: Persuade, inside the portfolio's Experience world.
Audience and job: freelance and agency clients deciding in seconds whether Ali can build or extend their product; they want to see what a business gets and why it can be trusted.
Proof and content: the EazyCRM facts in `app/data/seed.ts` (three figures, the seven pipeline stages in the resume's words). App screenshots and any extra numbers come from the owner through the admin; none are invented, and an empty gallery renders nothing.
Constraints: the desk identity is binding (see PRODUCT.md); editable from the admin; works without script.
Unresolved: EazyCRM app screenshots; further metrics.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A client can watch their customer's WhatsApp message travel through the product, carried by the desk's own paper plane, with each stop saying in plain words what it protects. The page refuses the usual case-study template of a screenshot, a paragraph and a tag list.
OWN-WORLD: The existing desk: near-black ground and paper theme, amber outline on panel-filled doodles, cyan fold accents, JetBrains Mono and Courier Prime for labels, Caveat for handwriting. The paper plane is the message; stops are drawn nodes on one amber route, not a row of equal cards.
STORY: A client understands what they get (one inbox, campaigns, store link, their own data), believes it by following the message end to end, then emails Ali.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Existing project hero (title, summary, email action), directly followed by a four-item "what your business gets" list set as plain divided rows; the route diagram is the next screen's thesis.
FORM: extension of the existing project page, no concept roll; position: none; seed: none (pinned) after the owner's words "Yes, start it" and "EazyCRM flagship case study".
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
